main quest

Dhīr. Two models, zero borrowed weights.

Dhīr 2B (dense) and Dhīr 5B MoE were pretrained from random initialisation on Google Cloud TPU v6e, on 6T+ tokens, with a tokenizer built in-house for English and ten Indian languages. Launched 3 November 2026 under the IndiaAI Mission. Launch coverage calls them India's first billion-parameter generative models. I'm second author on the technical report, among 17.

The trail

Dhīr is a big team's work. This is the stack, and the places I've left fingerprints in it. Hover a stage.

  1. 01Datacurate · dedupe · shard

    Pipelines on datatrove and NeMo Curator. Doc-prep tooling for papers, tables and formulas.

  2. 02Tokenizer131k vocab · 11 languages

    Compression studied across twelve tokenizers on Indic, English, maths and code.

  3. 03PretrainJAX · MaxText · XPK

    Sharding layouts, TPU recipes, checkpoint lineage hashed from step 0. Notes in the research book.

  4. 04Mid-train + SFTinstruction tuning

    Instruction-tuning pipelines on DeepSpeed, FSDP and Ray, with activation recomputation.

  5. 05AlignRLHF · GRPO · DPO

    RL on TPUs: 2,048 answers scored per step, language-aware rewards, reward-hacking checks.

  6. 06Ship4-bit · llama.cpp · edge

    Quantised builds running on laptops, Jetson boards and Raspberry Pi class hardware.
