Across the LLM stack

I've worked across the LLM stack, from data and tokenisation to model architecture, large-scale pretraining, post-training and deployment. I enjoy the messy side of engineering as much as the breakthroughs, especially when a training run finally works after hours of debugging.

03 · Featured project

Dhīr: foundation models from scratch

Dhīr 2B (dense) and Dhīr 5B MoE were pretrained from random initialisation on more than 6 trillion tokens, with an in-house tokenizer for English and ten Indian languages. Both run entirely offline on laptops and mobile hardware. I'm second author on the technical report, among 17 authors.
