Featured project

Dhīr: language models pretrained from scratch

Dhīr 2B (dense) and Dhīr 5B MoE start from random initialisation and train on more than 6 trillion tokens on Google Cloud TPU v6e, using a tokenizer built in-house for English and ten Indian languages. They launched on 3 November 2026 under the IndiaAI Mission, and launch coverage calls them India's first billion-parameter generative models. I'm second author on the technical report, among 17 authors.

Across the stack

Dhīr is the work of a large team. This is the pipeline, and the parts of it I've worked on. Hover or tap a stage for detail.

  1. 01Datacurate, dedupe, shard

    Pipelines on datatrove and NeMo Curator, plus tooling to turn papers, tables and formulas into training data.

  2. 02Tokenizer131k vocab, 11 languages

    Comparing compression across twelve tokenizers on Indian languages, English, maths and code.

  3. 03PretrainingJAX, MaxText, XPK

    Sharding layouts, TPU recipes, restarts, and a checkpoint lineage hashed from step 0. Notes in the research book.

  4. 04Mid-training + SFTinstruction tuning

    Instruction-tuning pipelines on DeepSpeed, FSDP and Ray, with activation recomputation and mixed precision.

  5. 05AlignmentRLHF, GRPO, DPO

    RL on TPUs: 2,048 answers scored per step, language-aware rewards, and checks for reward hacking.

  6. 06Deployment4-bit, llama.cpp, edge

    Quantised builds running on laptops, Jetson boards and Raspberry Pi class hardware.
